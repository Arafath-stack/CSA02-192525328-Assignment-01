When does double lose accuracy? 2^53 = 9007199254740992
  (double)2^53 + 1 == 2^53 ? yes - the +1 is lost
  district total is about 4.1e10 paise, far below 2^53 (9.0e15): double stays exact for integers here
  but double rupees with fractions (0.01) are NOT exact, so error accumulates
